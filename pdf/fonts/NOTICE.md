# Fonts bundled with ufo

## WenQuanYi Micro Hei

`wqy-microhei.ttc` is WenQuanYi Micro Hei 0.2.0-beta, unmodified, from
<https://downloads.sourceforge.net/project/wqy/wqy-microhei/0.2.0-beta/wqy-microhei-0.2.0-beta.tar.gz>
(SHA-256 of the file:
`2420e8078af796b19a3f6ef13de527a1a91c1e7171eea115926c614ced1009b3`).

The collection holds two faces. ufo uses the first, WenQuanYi Micro Hei. It
has glyphs for Latin, Greek and Cyrillic text, kana, the unified Han
characters U+4E00-U+9FC3 and Hangul syllables. It has none for Hebrew, Arabic,
Thai or the scripts of India.
